# Code of conduct

English | [简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh.md)

## Summary

Keep project discussions respectful, specific, and useful. Critique ideas and code with evidence; respect different experience levels and backgrounds.

## Participation

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, and disclosure of another person’s private information are unacceptable. Respect requests to stop disruptive behavior.

Maintainers may remove disruptive content or restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately to `magolorcz@gmail.com`, with relevant context and your preferred contact method.
