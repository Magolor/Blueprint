# Development log entry

## Summary

Summarize the meaningful outcome and verification state. Follow the existing
log's date/order, metadata, and entry shape. For a compact rolling log, the
sections below may be fields rather than headings. Keep routine entries brief. For growing history, use the repository’s year/month/date layout behind one compact entry point; do not create a parallel log or migrate its format incidentally.

## Changed

Identify the task or direct request. Explain the behavior or decision and why
it matters. Link its canonical owner; record a rejected experiment only when
its evidence is useful for future work.

## Verified

Record actual checks, results, tested revision, and material gaps. Keep transcripts
and exploratory notes in linked evidence or scratch owners. Per-slice details belong here or in that evidence, not in the high-level decision list. Promote lasting conclusions into canonical docs.

## Next

Link the task or write none. For a handoff, identify the first unfinished
acceptance condition and link its owner. This entry records history, not live state.

## References

Link durable conclusions and supporting artifacts when not already linked above.
Do not create another log, schema, or duplicated plan.
