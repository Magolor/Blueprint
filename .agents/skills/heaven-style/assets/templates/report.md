# Report

## Summary

Summarize the subject, principal result, and practical implication. Identify
provisional conclusions and use the repository's report metadata.

## Scope and method

Explain the question, affected system, revision or environment, method, and
material exclusions.

## Findings

Present evidence in order of consequence. Explain reader impact before technical
detail and distinguish observation from hypothesis. Add a later technical
subsection only when it supports the conclusion.

## Conclusions and next steps

State what the evidence establishes, its limits, and the recommended action.
Identify the follow-up owner and staleness trigger. Promote accepted durable
conclusions to their canonical owner rather than leaving this report authoritative.

## References

Link exact evidence, source revisions, and relevant canonical docs.
