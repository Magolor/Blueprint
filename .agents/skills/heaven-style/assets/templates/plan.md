# <Topic> Plan

## Summary

Summarize the outcome and execution approach. Use the repository's required
metadata; when no format exists, identify the owner, status, scope, and canonical
task. Link the accepted design instead of repeating its rationale.

## Outcome and boundaries

Locate the affected feature and state observable success criteria, constraints,
and non-goals. Distinguish accepted scope from unresolved decisions.

## Steps

Use a short sequence for small work, or repeat the following slice for work
that needs independent review or handoff:

### Slice: <outcome>

- Work: concrete steps, responsible modules, and required inputs.
- Acceptance: observable completion conditions.
- Verification: exact commands or manual checks and expected evidence.
- Docs: canonical claims and projections to update.

## Dependencies and checkpoints

State sequencing, review points, and conditions that require revising the plan.
Add migration, rollout, or rollback only when applicable. Keep live state in
the canonical queue and one linked plan rather than duplicate trackers.

## Closeout

Record actual verification, material gaps, and follow-up or none when closing
or handing off work. While planning, state the closeout criteria instead.

## References

Link the task, design, contracts, and evidence needed to execute the work.
