# Proofread

## Summary

Check the whole artifact for accurate meaning, useful emphasis, and clear language. Preserve its audience and purpose while correcting local defects.

## Procedure

Read the complete artifact and the owners of material claims. Check the opening summary, current behavior, ordering, terminology, examples, links, and applicable template sections. Introduce internal names through their role. Preserve conditions, quantities, exceptions, and uncertainty.

Use [language](../rules/project/language.md), [claim verification](../rules/project/docs/prose.md), and [correction integration](../rules/project/docs/edit.md). Remove correction history and disproportionate detail from current guides; record necessary history in its owner. Do not add sections merely to discuss the latest edit.

Run relevant link, snippet, and document checks. If proofreading is read-only, report findings; otherwise make authorized corrections and reread the affected page as a whole.

## Acceptance

A new reader can identify the purpose, understand the supported claims, and find the next useful source. Material factual gaps are explicit; optional style preferences do not block completion. No word-count validator is required.
